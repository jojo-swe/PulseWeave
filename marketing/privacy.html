Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 8, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to PulseWeave ("we", "us", "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our team communication platform and related services.

2. Data We Collect

We collect and process the following categories of personal data:

  • Account Data — email address, display name, username, password (hashed), and profile picture.
  • Workspace Data — workspace names, channel names, membership, and roles.
  • Content Data — messages, files, and media you share within the platform.
  • Technical Data — IP address, browser type, device information, and access logs.
  • Usage Data — feature usage patterns, session duration, and interaction metrics.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the PulseWeave service.
  • Authenticate your identity and manage your account.
  • Deliver messages and notifications you request.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate service updates.
  • Detect, prevent, and address security issues and abuse.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Legal Basis (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, we process your data under the following legal bases:

  • Contract — processing necessary to provide the service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate Interests — improving our service, security, and fraud prevention.
  • Consent — where you have given explicit consent (e.g., marketing emails).
  • Legal Obligation — compliance with applicable laws.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Account data is retained while your account is active and for 30 days after deletion request.
  • Message content is retained according to your workspace settings or until you delete it.
  • Technical logs are retained for up to 90 days for security and debugging purposes.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • TLS encryption for data in transit.
  • Encryption at rest for sensitive data.
  • Secure password hashing (bcrypt/argon2).
  • Role-based access controls and audit logging.
  • Regular security assessments and updates.

7. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential cookies to authenticate sessions and remember preferences. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw Consent — withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@pulseweave.com.

9. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for payment processing (Stripe), email delivery, and infrastructure hosting. These providers have their own privacy policies and are contractually obligated to protect your data.

10. Children's Privacy

PulseWeave is not intended for users under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, contact us at:

privacy@pulseweave.com
